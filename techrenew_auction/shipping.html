Shipping

Buyers arrange their own shipping. Shipping isn't included in the price of a lot — you choose the carrier and pay them directly, so you can use whichever service works best for you.

How it works

  • Pay for your lot. See Payment for the options.
  • We send the size and weight. Once your payment is confirmed, we'll email you the packed dimensions and weight of your lot.
  • You arrange shipping. Use those to book shipping with the carrier of your choice, then send us the details.
  • We hand it over. We'll have the lot ready for your carrier to collect.

Questions about shipping? Contact us — we're happy to help.
