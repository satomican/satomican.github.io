Payment

You don't pay anything when you make an offer. If we accept it, we'll email you payment links for the accepted amount. All prices are in US dollars.

Payment methods

  • Bank transfer (ACH) — recommended, no processing fee. Usually takes 1–2 business days to clear.
  • Credit card — Visa, Mastercard and American Express. A processing fee of 2.9% + $0.30 is added to the total. This fee isn't refundable.

Both are processed securely by Stripe. Your card and bank details go straight to Stripe — we never see or store them.

When to pay

Please pay within 3 business days of your offer being accepted. If we don't receive payment by then, we may release the lot back to other buyers.

Questions about paying? Contact us — we're happy to help.
